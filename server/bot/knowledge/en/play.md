# Playing a board

Above the board there is a row of tools. The turned-back arrow is Undo, the bin clears the board and always asks first, and the bulb is the hint. Undo and the bulb grey out when they cannot help, and some boards have no Undo.

The bulb gives a hint when you are stuck, and on many boards it also says why. A board allows a few hints for each puzzle. When they are spent, a short video gives one more.

On most boards a mistake costs nothing on Easy, Medium and Hard: those levels cannot be lost. Hearts are rare now. Four Shelves gives four hearts a day and a wrong guess costs one, and Golden Acorn and How Big? give two hearts on Insane.

On many boards the Insane level has a limited number of moves instead, shown as a count of moves left. Every piece you put down costs a move, and notes and flags are free.

Stuck on a board is normal. Ask what the rule is, try the bulb, or leave it and come back tomorrow, since a new puzzle comes every day.
