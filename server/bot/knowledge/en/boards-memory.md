# Memory boards

Glimpse. A little picture shows for a few seconds. Rebuild it bead by bead from memory. Its motto: "Look once, remember".

Birdsong. A woodpecker knocks a rhythm on the old tree. Knock it back on the drum; each round it adds one knock. Its motto: "Listen, then knock it back".

Teacups. Cups upside down on the tea table, a little creature hiding under each. Lift two at a time and find every pair of twins. Its motto: "Remember where you saw it".
