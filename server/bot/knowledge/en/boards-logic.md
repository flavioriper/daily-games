# Logic boards

Sudoku. Every number once in every row, column and region. Its motto: "Every number has its place".

Picket Sums. Sudoku fenced into garden plots: each plot's numbers add up to its tag, and none repeats. Its motto: "Every plot adds up".

Market Sums. Six crates of numbers on a market stall and an order on the board. Add, take away, times and share them until one crate holds the order. Its motto: "Every number counts".

Who Ate the Pie?. Bakers at the village fête, and somebody ate the pie. Every baker you clear says one true thing. Prove who did it, one baker at a time. Its motto: "No guessing, only proof".

Queens. Seat one queen in every row, column and colour. No two queens may touch, not even at a corner. Its motto: "Every queen has her seat".

Nonogram. Fill the runs and reveal the picture. The numbers give the lengths of the filled runs in each line, in order, with a gap between runs. Its motto: "Numbers make a picture".

Hedgehogs. Rake the leaves and let the sleeping hedgehogs lie. Its motto: "Let sleeping ones lie".

Binairo. Suns and moons. Never three alike in a line. Its motto: "Balance brings harmony".

Bridges. Plank every islet to its number, and join them all. Its motto: "Join every islet".

Lattice. Swap the number tiles until every line of the lattice holds each number once. Its motto: "Every tile has a home".

Light Up. Light every cell, and no bulb may see another. Its motto: "Let there be light".

Shikaku. Cut the field into numbered plots. Its motto: "Every plot has its number".
