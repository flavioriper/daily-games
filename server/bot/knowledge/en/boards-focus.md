# Focus boards

Jewel Box. Lift the jewels off the wrong slots and set each one where its colour belongs. Its motto: "A place for every jewel".

Yarn Hoop. Thread the pins of the hoop and knit a hidden picture, panel by panel. Its motto: "Pin to pin, stitch by stitch".

Pixel Garden. Copy the picture onto the pegboard one bead at a time. Its motto: "Bead by bead".
