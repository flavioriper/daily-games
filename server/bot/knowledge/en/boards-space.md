# Space boards

Quilt. Fit every patch onto the quilt, with not a gap left. Its motto: "Make the blanket whole".

Hosepipes. Taps and flowers on a raised garden bed. Drag a hose from each tap to its flower: hoses never cross, and every square must be watered. Its motto: "Water every square".

Paper Box. A flat paper net lies on the mat with boxes under it. Pick the box it folds into, five times a day. Its motto: "Fold it in your head".

Mini Golf. Putt the ball round the day's course in as few strokes as you can. Its motto: "Fewest strokes wins".

Fairy Lights. Turn the wire until every lantern is lit. Its motto: "Wake every lantern".

Untangle. Lift the pegs over the ropes until every rope comes free. Its motto: "Every knot comes undone".

Horse Pen. Pen the horse in with hay bales, and keep as much meadow as you can. Its motto: "Pen the wandering horse".

Pinwheel. Turn each pinned piece until the frame is full. Its motto: "Turn it till it fits".

Sunbeam. Slide the mirrors and bend the light to the bud. Its motto: "Every drop, then the bud".
