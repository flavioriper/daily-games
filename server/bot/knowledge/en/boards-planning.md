# Planning boards

Rings. Gather every colour onto a peg of its own. Its motto: "Every ring finds its peg".

Paper Planes. Tap a plane whose lane to the edge is clear, and off it goes. Its motto: "A clear lane and away".

Super Slider. Slide the blocks and get the big red one out of the gate. Its motto: "Out through the gate".

Caterpillar. Walk every square, eating the leaves in order. Its motto: "Leaf by leaf, every square".
