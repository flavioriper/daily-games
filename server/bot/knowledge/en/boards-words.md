# Word boards

Hidden Word. Five letters, six tries. A new word every day. On Hard and Insane every clue must be used: a green letter stays in its place, and a letter the rows found goes in every guess after. Its motto: "Find the hidden word".

Word Trail. Trace every hidden word. The lengths are the only clue. Its motto: "Every letter finds its way".

Honeycomb. Seven letters on a comb, the queen bee on the middle one. Spell words that hold her letter and pour their honey into the jar. Its motto: "Every word is honey".

Four Shelves. Jam jars on the pantry dresser hide shelves of four words that go together. Find every shelf. Its motto: "Four that belong together".

Cross Stitch. Six words cross on a sampler, their letters sewn on in the wrong places. Swap two at a time until every letter is stitched. Its motto: "Every letter in its place".

Padlock. A padlock with letter dials. Turn them so the window spells words; light every letter on every dial and the lock opens. Its motto: "Every letter lit, the lock opens".

Biscuit Tin. A tin of broken biscuits, letters iced on every piece. Lay pieces on the plate to spell words; four that spell a long word make a whole biscuit. Its motto: "Every biscuit whole, the tin is done".

Stepping Stones. A brook with a word on each bank. Lay stepping stones across, each a word one letter from the last, and reach the far bank in as few as you can. Its motto: "One letter at a time, across the brook".

Garden Fence. A garden bed with a letter on every fence post. String twine post to post to spell words, each starting where the last ended, until every post is in bloom. Its motto: "Every post in bloom".

Five Clues. Five clue cards turn one at a time, each a thing that belongs to one hidden idea. Name the idea with as few as you can. Its motto: "The fewer clues, the better".

Golden Acorn. A daily quiz. Four answers, one right: pick, lock, and learn why. Its motto: "Are you sure?".

How Big?. Two silhouettes, one at its real size. Pull the other until it looks right beside it. Its motto: "Size it by eye".

Pearl Dive. Name a kind of thing against the clock. The rarer your answer, the deeper the dive. Its motto: "The rarer, the deeper.".
