Privacy Policy

Peeplet Daily Puzzles, for Android and iOS. Last updated 28 September 2026.

Who we are

Peeplet Daily Puzzles ("the game") is published by Hypertradeworx ("we"). If you have a question about this policy or about your data, write to flavio@hypertradeworx.xyz. That address is also our contact for data protection matters (the LGPD's "encarregado").

What stays on your device

Your progress, streak, solve history, scores, settings (sound, language, reduce motion) and whether you bought Remove ads are saved on your device only. We do not upload them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What leaves your device, and why

1. Gameplay statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). To learn which puzzles are played and where players get stuck, the game sends events such as "a puzzle was started", "solved", "a hint was used", with the puzzle, difficulty, time taken, number of moves and the game's language. Each event carries a random ID made when the game is first opened and kept on your device. It identifies a copy of the game, not a person. We do not send your name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID with these events. Google receives your IP address when the events arrive, as with any internet request.

2. An anonymous account (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The game signs in with an anonymous account: a random user ID, with no email, name or password. It is used for the daily Trestle challenge, where your day's score (and the game's language) is stored so the game can show how your bridge compares with other players'. Only the combined figures are shown to other players.

3. Ads (Google AdMob). Unless you have bought Remove ads, the game shows a banner from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID (on iOS, the IDFA, only if you allow tracking when asked), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first, and ads are only personalised if you agree. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data. You can change these choices at any time from Settings > Privacy choices in the game (the button appears where these rules apply). How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. The Remove ads purchase. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase is owned, so it can hide the ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

Legal bases

For players covered by the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD: gameplay statistics and the anonymous account rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game; personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; the purchase is processed to perform our contract with you.

Who processes the data

Google LLC and its affiliates (Firebase, Google Analytics, Google Cloud, AdMob) process this data for us, on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States, under Google's standard contractual clauses. Apple and Google process purchases under their own policies. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Gameplay statistics are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted by Google Analytics. Daily challenge scores are kept while the feature runs and are tied only to the anonymous ID. AdMob data is kept according to Google's policies.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy choices, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings, or turn off tracking on iOS in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Buying Remove ads stops all ad requests.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device, including the link between you and the random IDs above.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Write to us at the address above. Because the IDs are random and not linked to your identity, we may be unable to find your data unless you can tell us the ID; we will help you as far as we can. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them was collected, contact us and we will delete what we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the game's store listing.

Nos estados dos EUA com leis de privacidade, você pode recusar a venda ou o compartilhamento dos seus dados. Você pode mudar essas escolhas a qualquer momento em Configurações > Opções de privacidade no jogo (o botão aparece onde essas regras se aplicam). Como o Google usa esses dados: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. A compra de Remover anúncios. As compras são feitas inteiramente pelo Google Play ou pela App Store. Nunca vemos seus dados de pagamento. O jogo recebe apenas a confirmação de que a compra é sua, para esconder os anúncios e restaurar a compra em outro aparelho.

Por quanto tempo guardamos

As estatísticas de jogo são guardadas por até 14 meses e depois apagadas pelo Google Analytics. As pontuações do desafio diário ficam guardadas enquanto o recurso existir e estão ligadas apenas ao ID anônimo. Os dados do AdMob são guardados conforme as políticas do Google.

  • Desinstalar o jogo apaga tudo o que está guardado no aparelho, inclusive a ligação entre você e os IDs aleatórios acima.
  • Pela LGPD você tem direito a confirmar o tratamento, acessar, corrigir, anonimizar, apagar ou portar seus dados, a se opor ao tratamento e a revogar o consentimento. Escreva para o endereço acima. Como os IDs são aleatórios e não estão ligados à sua identidade, talvez não consigamos encontrar seus dados sem que você nos informe o ID; ajudaremos no que for possível. Você também pode reclamar à ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).

Crianças

O jogo é destinado a jogadores a partir de 13 anos e não é dirigido a crianças. Não coletamos intencionalmente dados de menores de 13 anos. Se você acredita que uma criança usou o jogo e teve dados coletados, fale conosco e apagaremos o que for possível.

Alterações
