Privacy Policy

Tally Factory, for Android. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Who we are

Tally Factory ("the game") is published by Hypertradeworx ("we"). If you have a question about this policy or about your data, write to flavio@hypertradeworx.xyz. That address is also our contact for data protection matters (the LGPD's "encarregado").

What stays on your device

Your factory, your level and upgrades, your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics) and whether you own the full game are saved on your device only. We do not upload them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

What the game does not do

The game has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics of our own. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. It can be played with no internet connection.

What leaves your device, and why

The one purchase (the full factory). The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. When the game starts, and when you buy or restore the purchase, it asks Google Play for the price and for whether the purchase is owned by the signed-in store account, so that it can unlock the full game and restore it on a new device. Apart from the third-party components below, the game makes no other connection.

Third-party components. The game is built with the Unity engine and uses Google Play Billing for its purchase. These components may process technical information about your device, such as its model and operating system version, as described in their own policies: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy and policies.google.com/privacy.

Legal bases

For players covered by the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD: the purchase is processed to perform our contract with you. We process nothing else.

Who processes the data

Google processes purchases under its own policies, on servers that may be outside your country. We do not sell your data.

Your choices and rights

  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device.
  • Refunds and purchase history are managed in your Google Play account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no data about you, there is normally nothing for us to provide or delete; write to us at the address above and we will help you as far as we can. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

Deleting your data

Uninstall the game. Everything it saved is on your device and is deleted with it. Nothing about you is kept on our side.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
