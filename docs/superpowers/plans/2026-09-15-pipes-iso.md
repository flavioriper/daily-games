# Pipes as an island of blocks -- implementation plan

*Plan for `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-15-pipes-iso-design.md`, 2026-09-15.
One branch, `feat/pipes-iso`. Tasks 2, 3 and 4 touch disjoint files and are
dispatched in parallel; tasks 1 and 5 are the board's own two halves and are
written in order, because 5 is the only caller of 1.*

Every task ends the same way: `godot --headless --path . --script
res://tests/run_tests.gd` green, and no change to a file another task owns.

## Task 1 -- the rules and the generator (`puzzles/pipes_iso_gen.gd`)

A new pure `RefCounted`, headless, no nodes. It owns:

* **Directions.** `N = 1` (-Z), `E = 2` (+X), `S = 4` (+Z), `W = 8` (-X),
  `U = 16` (+Y), `D = 32` (-Y); `DIRS` (the six bits in that order),
  `STEP` (bit -> `Vector3i`), `OPPOSITE`, `HORIZONTAL` (the four side bits).
* **Rotations.** `rotations()` -> the 24 axis-aligned `Basis` values with
  determinant +1, built once and cached; `rotate_mask(mask, basis)` maps every
  bit's `STEP` through the basis and reads the bit back;
  `orientations(reference)` -> the distinct masks reachable from a kind's
  reference mask, each with the basis that reaches it, in a stable order.
* **Kinds.** `KINDS = ["straight", "elbow", "tee", "pump"]`, `REFERENCE`
  (kind -> mask, matching `core/placeholders.gd` and `art/pipes.blend`),
  `SLOT` (kind -> model slot), and `mask_kind(mask)` -> the kind a mouth set
  needs, or `""`.
* **Terrain.** heights as `Array` of `Array` (`[z][x]`), `height_at`,
  `is_air(cell)`, `crown(x, z)` -> the cell resting on that column.
* **The standing rule.** `stands(cell, mask, heights)`.
* **Flow.** `flow(pieces, heights, source) -> {"fed": {cell: depth},
  "runs": [...], "leaks": [...]}` where `pieces` is `{Vector3i: {"kind":,
  "mask":}}`. Vertical runs first (a maximal stack joined by U/D mouths, with
  `has_pump`), then a breadth-first search from the source: level and downward
  steps free, an upward step only inside a pumped run. `leaks(...)` returns
  fed mouths that meet nothing, as `{"cell":, "bit":}`, nearest the source
  first.
* **The generator.** `generate(rng, difficulty) -> Dictionary` with `cols`,
  `rows`, `levels`, `heights`, `source`, `source_mask`, `drains` (cells),
  `drain_masks`, `route` (ordered cells), `solution` (cell -> {kind, mask}),
  `tray` (kind -> count), `hidden` (how many route cells the check found
  hidden) and `ok`. Spec section 8 has the algorithm and the difficulty table.
* **The verifier.** `verify(out) -> bool`, run inside `generate` before it
  returns: every drain fed, no leaks, every piece standing, every climb
  pumped. `ok = false` after a bounded number of attempts, the way
  `horse_gen.generate` gives up.
* **Occlusion.** `hidden_cells(route, heights, cols, rows) -> int`, marching
  from each route cell toward the camera along `VIEW_DIR` (the first stop's
  own direction, `pitch 35`, `yaw 45`) and counting the ones that pass under a
  crown.

Do not touch any other file. `tests/test_pipes.gd` is rewritten in task 7.

## Task 2 -- the four models' placeholders and their contract rows

Files: `core/placeholders.gd`, `core/models.gd`, `tests/test_models.gd`,
`tools/blender_export.py`, `docs/art/blender-contract.md`.

`block`, `pump`, `source_tank` and `drain_pool` as primitive stand-ins in the
house style (`_layer` / `_merge` / `_bar` / `_ball` / `_cylinder`, one mesh per
layer, base at y = 0, `Toon.apply_to` at the end), registered in
`Models.SLOTS` in that order after `valve`, with the layer names and budgets
from the spec's section 9. `Models._dress` gives `drain_pool`'s `Water_flat`
the stage water material (as `channel` does) and turns off the shadow on every
`_flat` layer that lies inside another's silhouette. `block` goes in the
exporter's `EXACT` table at 1.0 x 1.0.

## Task 3 -- an orthographic rig that turns

Files: `world/camera_rig.gd`, `world/stage.gd`, `core/puzzle_base_3d.gd`.

* `CameraRig` gains `orthographic: bool`, `fit()` branching to an analytic
  `_fit_ortho` (spec section 6), and `turn(steps: int) -> Tween` tweening
  `yaw_deg` by a quarter per step over 0.35 s eased, re-placing the camera
  every frame of it and re-fitting at the end.
* The ortho fit sizes for the largest extent over the four yaw stops, so the
  island keeps its size at every stop.
* `Stage.fit_camera(aabb, rect, pitch, projection, yaw)` passes the two new
  arguments through, both with defaults that leave every other board exactly
  as it is today.
* `PuzzleBase3D` gains `board_projection() -> int` (`Camera3D.PROJECTION_*`,
  default perspective), `board_yaw() -> float` (default NAN) and
  `local_ray(local: Vector2) -> Array` returning `[origin, direction]` for a
  control-local point, so a board can pick without a plane.

Perspective boards must come out of this byte-identical in behaviour: the win
harness's `camera fit=true` for all twelve is the check.

## Task 4 -- the piece tray in the HUD

Files: `ui/hud/piece_tray.gd` (new), `ui/hud/action_bar.gd`, `ui/icons.gd`,
`core/puzzle_base.gd`, `ui/puzzle_host.gd`.

* `PuzzleBase` gains the optional interface: `pieces() -> Array[Dictionary]`
  (`{"kind": String, "count": int, "selected": bool, "enabled": bool}`),
  `pick(i)` already exists and selects one, `turn_view()` and
  `peek(on: bool)`.
* Two new capabilities: `"pieces"` shows the `PieceTray` (in the action bar,
  where the colour tray goes -- the same carved wooden trough, one button per
  kind, the piece's icon over its count, the selected one outlined in `SUN`),
  and `"view"` adds a turn button and a peek button beside Reset.
* `ui/icons.gd` gains `pipe_straight`, `pipe_elbow`, `pipe_tee`, `pipe_pump`,
  `turn` and `eye`, drawn as polylines in the unit square like the rest.
* The host wires the tray's `pick` to `_puzzle.pick`, the turn button to
  `turn_view()` and the peek button's `button_down` / `button_up` to
  `peek(true)` / `peek(false)`.

Every other board must keep its HUD unchanged: the capability gates do that.

## Task 5 -- the board (`puzzles/pipes_iso.gd`)

The only file that knows about all of the above. Sections, in build order:

1. `build()`: the generator, then the scene -- merged ground, source tank,
   drains, the mouth spheres, `Fx`, the tray's counts.
2. Picking: `_gui_input` overridden to ray-pick mouths and crowns
   (`local_ray`), with a long-press timer for removal.
3. Placement: `_place(cell, mask)`, `_cycle(cell)`, `_lift(cell)`, each
   pushing an entry on the history.
4. The flood: `_flood()` carried over from the pad board -- depth-staggered
   wet tweens on `Steel`, `Collar` and the `wet` uniform, `_run_jets()` for
   the leaks, the drain's fill.
5. Undo, hint (the route's next piece, locked), reset.
6. Peek, turn, and the entrance and win from the spec's section 10.

## Task 6 -- the models in Blender

`art/pipes.blend` gains `Block`, `Pump`, `Source_Tank` and `Drain_Pool` as
collections, modelled in the live session through the Blender MCP (never a
script that rebuilds the file), exported through
`tools/blender_export.py`, re-imported, and looked at with
`tests/_shot_model.gd` before being called done. `tools/build_models.sh`
learns the four names.

## Task 7 -- the seams

* `ui/registry.gd`: the `pipes` entry points at `puzzles/pipes_iso.gd`, with
  the blurb "Build the pipeline. Water won't climb without a pump."
* `tests/test_pipes.gd`: rewritten around the new generator -- rotations are
  24 and distinct, `rotate_mask` round-trips, the orientation counts per kind
  match the spec's table, the standing rule, flow with and without a pump,
  a leak found, and `generate` at all three difficulties verifying.
* `tests/_win.gd`: `_solve_pipes` replays the route through tap-a-mouth.
* `puzzles/pipes3d.gd` and `puzzles/pipes_gen.gd` deleted.
* `CLAUDE.md`'s analytics list gains `view_turn` and `peek_used`; `README.md`
  and the concept page record what was built.
