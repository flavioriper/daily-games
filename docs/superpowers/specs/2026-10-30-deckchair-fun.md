# Deckchair: better to play, more fun

The user, 2026-10-30: way better to play, way funnier, nobody likes it now;
in-game UI as the daily puzzles'; breathing room at the borders; a tap only
sends a card home; no auto-finish.

Why it was not liked: half of random deals beaten by the shuffle, a framed
small table, nothing to chase but a score, and tapping everything played it.

1. Laid as a daily board: world edge to edge, top bar, tools row.
2. Every deal comes from a bank of deals the bot won unaided.
3. No difficulty levels: the daily was the reference for the UI only.
4. The deckchair: one card may rest in the top row's spare place.
5. A streak up to x5 on good moves; undo ends it, idle stock turns cool it.
6. A tap sends a card home and nowhere else; everything else is carried.
7. No automatic finish.
8. The bulb reads first for free, then spends a hint to ring the move.
