The four cheap shapes

Every low-cost success on the board is one of these four. The mock under each title is what the player actually looks at.

1 · Pure numbers

Antimatter Dimensions · Revolution Idle

On screen
Rows of numbers and buy buttons. No art at all.
Proof
Antimatter Dimensions 1M+ at 4.8; Revolution Idle 500K+ at 4.8, launched Oct 2024.
Money
IAP only, no ads. Players in this niche punish ads.

Build cost
Cheap to draw, expensive to design: the whole game is prestige layers and balance curves. Months of tuning, no assets.

2 · Text and UI

Idle Obelisk Miner · Magic Research · Universal Paperclips

On screen
A log, some tabs, numbers. Obelisk Miner looks like DOS in 1995 on purpose.
Proof
Obelisk Miner 1M+ at 4.8 and roughly $1.5M a month; Magic Research 100K+ as a paid $7.99 app.
Money
Either deep IAP with no ads, or premium one-off.

Build cost
No art pipeline at all, but the content is writing and systems, and it never stops. Obelisk Miner took three years before the money moved.

3 · One screen, one mechanic

Bee Factory · Terrarium · Gun Idle · Idle Human · Idle Ants

On screen
One cute object, two or three upgrade buttons, a rewarded-ad button.
Proof
Green Panda run five of these at 10M+ installs each — Bee Factory 595K ratings, Terrarium 453K, Sushi Bar 396K.
Money
Ads everywhere plus a remove-ads subscription. Volume business.

Build cost
The one that maps onto your Godot toon pipeline almost unchanged. Cost is art polish and ad plumbing, not systems.

4 · Idle on top of a toy

Scratch Inc. · Pincremental · Idle Dot Shooter

On screen
A small satisfying toy on the left, the upgrade stack on the right.
Proof
Idle Dot Shooter hit 1M+ installs in seven months from a Feb 2026 launch; Scratch Inc. ships in 38 MB, solo, no ads.
Money
Mixed. Scratch Inc. sells IAP with no ads; Dot Shooter runs ads.

Build cost
The cheapest real shot: the toy is a weekend, the idle stack on top is the known part, and the novelty is what gets it noticed.

What the money actually looks like

The two ends of the same "cheap to build" bracket, fifty times apart. Both are one person. Neither bought users.

Idle Obelisk Miner · Checkbox Entertainment · one developer
$1.5M / month

Pure text, DOS-1995 look, mono chiptune, no tutorial, no hints and no ads at all. Sat at $30–40K a day for three years, then went vertical once the systems were deep enough to hold people.

The other end: Magic Research

Also one person. Also text-only. Also praised — 4.7 on Play, sold as a $7.99 premium app with no ads, marketed with two Reddit posts and nothing else.

A Steam revenue calculator puts its Steam gross at about $31K lifetime. Third-party write-ups claim $150K in eight months and $400K in twelve across all stores — those are estimates from outside the developer, so treat the spread as the honest range, not a number.

Read it this way: the cheap shapes have a very long tail. Same cost, same craft, same genre, and the outcome spans two orders of magnitude. What separates them is years of content depth and a community, not the build.

The catch that matters for you. An incremental is the opposite shape of a daily. A daily wins on a two-minute session and a fresh board tomorrow; an incremental wins on six-month retention and a content treadmill that never stops. Obelisk Miner needed three years of patches before the revenue moved. The build is the small part.

Where not to go, and what is open

LaneWho owns itVerdict
Tycoon / merge / hero-RPG idle Kolibri, Codigames, AppQuantum, Rollic, Game Hive. Recent App Store entries in this lane ship at 300 MB – 3.4 GB. Closed. It runs on art volume, live ops and paid user acquisition. Nothing you can out-build alone.
One screen, one mechanic Green Panda hold the template, but the shelf is wide: Idle Mow, Will it Crush, Planet Evolution and others all sit on it with no studio behind them. Open, on the condition that the object on screen is charming. That is exactly what your Blender-to-toon pipeline already produces.
Pure numbers / text Solo developers, every one of them: Kajfosz, Checkbox, Oni Gaming, Maticolotto. Open and proven, but it is a years-long content commitment and the audience is r/incremental_games, not the store charts.
Idle bolted onto a toy Thin. Scratch cards, pinball and a turret are taken; most toys are not. The best risk-to-reward on this page. Small build, novelty does the marketing, and you can test whether the loop holds before committing a year.
One more data point worth holding onto. Idle Mow: Grass Cutting Tycoon sits at 100K+ installs and 4.6, and Zplay's older Idle Grass Cutter has 31.5K App Store ratings with its last update in April 2019. A one-mechanic idler can be shipped and left alone. None of the other three shapes can.

What ports from daily as it stands

You already haveWhat it would do in an idler
Blender one-mesh-per-layer pipeline + toon shader + outline passThe whole of archetype 3. The hero object on screen is the game, and recolouring a layer per upgrade tier is free in this setup.
ui/hud/model_view.gd SubViewport stageUpgrade cards that each show the real model rather than an icon — the thing that makes these games look expensive.
core/analytics.gd (GA4 Measurement Protocol)Retention and funnel measurement, which is the only number that matters in this genre. Already wired, already shipping.
core/backend.gd + Cloud Functions + anonymous authLeaderboards and cloud saves, both expected in an incremental. Save integrity is the one part you would have to harden.
Android export + CI + Firebase App DistributionShipping. Nothing new needed.
