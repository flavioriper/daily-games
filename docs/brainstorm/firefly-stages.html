Stages 1–15

The first block of the staged Firefly. Each stage is cleared on its own, can be replayed for a better score, and has four difficulties. There's no shop: every stage starts with the same gun, and a skilled player earns a buff for that stage by meeting its goal. These fifteen stages bring in the cast you already know, one thing at a time. Stage 16 brings the first new element.

Decided: stages, no upgrades, 4 difficulties, goals give buffsEvery number here is proposed and untested

Shared rules

The gun

The same on every stage: 1 damage, 3 volleys a second, 1 shot a volley. No energy, no shop. Only a buff changes it, and only until the stage ends.

Clearing

Shoot down the whole swarm to clear the stage. Losing your last firefly fails the attempt, and you retry the stage from its start.

Difficulties

Easy, Medium, Hard and Insane on every stage, each with its own best score. proposed All four are open on a stage you've reached, and clearing any of them opens the next stage.

Score

Points as today (gnat 50/100, ladybird 80/160, moth 150/400 and up, rogue 500/1,000, flyby 100 each). Replays are for a higher best. Extra fireflies from points are gone, since a stage is short.

The goal

One per stage, shown on the stage banner and as a small counter at the top of the field (for example 3 / 5). It's met once per attempt, and the buff starts at once.

How bugs are seated

Ten columns. Row 1 has the 4 moths (middle columns), rows 2–3 the 16 ladybirds (columns 2–9), rows 4–5 the 20 gnats (all ten). Each wave flies a looping path in, then each bug flies to its own seat.

Reading the wave pictures

Each wave is drawn on the field (240 × 372). This wave's bugs glow in the seats they fly to, bugs seated by earlier waves are faded, and the dashed line is the path the wave flies in on. On a flyby, the line is the path the bugs fly through and off the field.

Piercing sparkEach shot goes through the first bug it hits and can hit one more.

Slow seedsEvery seed falls at half speed.

For you to decide

Calls this page is for

  • Unlocks. Does clearing Easy open the next stage? Or does the next stage need Medium, so Easy is only practice?
  • Flybys in stages. Three in this block (3, 7, 11), keeping today's rhythm. They can't be failed except on Insane, so they're breathers. Fewer?
  • Extra fireflies from points. Dropped here. A stage is a minute or two, so the 20,000 mark would rarely be reached.
  • Boosters and Second chance. Spare firefly and Twin start still fit a stage attempt. Second chance would restart the stage with one firefly on the wave you reached. Keep them?
  • Stage 9's held firefly. It starts the stage with something to win back. Is that clear enough without a tutorial page?
  • The buffs. Are six enough for the block, and does any feel wrong for its stage?
  • What goes on the stage map. Four badges per stage (one per difficulty cleared) and the best score.
  • Stage 16's new element. A wasp that dives straight and fast was the first idea. Not designed here.

Next, once you've judged this: a bot pass per stage and difficulty to set the numbers, then the spec, then Godot.
