Memory as a sixth skill?

The brain report is built for five skills: core/brain.gd, the icons in ui/icons.gd, the SKILL_* rows and the five-spoke map on the win. Memory boards have no honest home today. Focus covers colour, detail and rhythm, and Words already claims recall.

add Memory. One icon, three locale rows, a sixth spoke. Next free colour in Pal.CAT is sky blue.

Arithmetic: its own skill, or inside Logic?

Domino Patio and Picket Sums are deduction with sums. Market Sums is pure arithmetic. All three could sit under Logic if Logic's line mentions numbers.

fold into Logic for now. Make Numbers a skill only if a third arithmetic board ships.

Left out on purpose: code-break, balance scales, tents, one-line drawing and mushroom patch genres, all removed on 2026-10-13 as not good enough, even though they are the cheapest to generate.
