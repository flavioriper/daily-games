The plots and the pieces
Why these rules

Everything is placement. A card is the only control and the plot it lands on is the whole decision, as in the farming game that started this. The turn fits one message on the Versus wire.

No damage numbers. A plant beside a bigger plant of the other side wilts a step; a wild eats a step. Three dots on a card is all the arithmetic there is.

The dens are the tower-defense half. Something comes out every third turn and heads for the nearest plant, whoever owns it. Hedges steer it, scarecrows send it off and pay the owner in cards. The two gardeners share the problem and can leave it to each other.

Numbers are untuned. Two steps a turn beside a pond, a sprout from every tree on even turns, three sun for a scarecrow: all placeholders until a canvas mock is played a few dozen times.
