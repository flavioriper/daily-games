Allotment Board Mock

You are Clay, bottom cottage. Sky plays the top one. Tap any plot, plant or wild to read its card. Tap a card in your hand, then a glowing plot, to play it. Next ends your turn: Sky plays, the wilds move, your plants grow.

How a turn goes

1 · Sun comes upYou get 2 sun, +1 for each meadow you hold. Draw a card, +1 per bramble patch where your bush stands.
2 · The garden movesYour plants shaded by a bigger neighbour of Sky's wilt a step. Then every plant of yours grows a step (two beside a pond). Apple trees drop a sprout on a free neighbour.
3 · You playSpend sun on cards. Seeds and hedges go on free ground touching ground you hold. Graft and the watering can go on your own plants.
4 · WildsAfter Sky's go, every wild steps toward the nearest plant and eats a step of whatever it stands on. Every third turn a den lets another one out. A scarecrow beside a wild sends it off, and its owner draws 2.
