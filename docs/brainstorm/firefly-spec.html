Identity

Rules of the name

Spec docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-arcade-firefly-design.md · notes docs/agents/arcade.md (Firefly)

The game follows a 1981 arcade formation shooter. The design spec names that game once, to forbid it. It is called Firefly and nothing else, in code, comments, commits and on screen. This page doesn't name the original either.

Built in one sitting from the brief “add a new arcade bottomnav, we gonna add some arcade solo games … Build it, include sfx.” It was the first game on the Arcade tab. Like every Arcade game it is not a Registry.PUZZLES entry and it has no levels. The run just gets harder as it goes.

What was kept from the arcade genre

  • A ship at the bottom that moves sideways. The arcade limit of two volleys in the air was dropped on 2026-10-06 for a fire rate.
  • A swarm of forty flies in along looping paths in five waves and takes its seats. It sways while it gathers and breathes in and out once it has.
  • Bugs peel off and dive in curves, firing, then come back in over the top. A boss dives with up to two escorts.
  • The capture beam, the rescue that makes a pair, and the captive that turns rogue.
  • A bonus stage on stage 3 and every fourth stage after it.
  • Extra ships at 20,000, 90,000 and every 70,000 after that. You start with three.

Hands

Controls

A slide, not a spot

Put a finger anywhere on the field. The firefly follows the slide's movement at 1.35× (GAIN), so the thumb never covers it and a small movement crosses the field. A slide past a wall re-anchors, so coming back moves at once.

Hold to fire

The gun fires for as long as the finger is down, at rate() volleys a second. The slide and the shots knock nothing (see haptics).

Keyboard

Arrows or A/D move; space fires.

Pause

Leaving the app, the settings sheet or the tutorial card pauses. A tap on the field resumes (“Tap to go on”).

Top bar: back, the title, Restart (grey until a run exists; a restart doesn't count the run in play) and settings. The settings sheet also has Start over, which wipes Firefly's arcade.cfg section (2026-10-17, ui/hud/start_over_sheet.gd). Android's back reaches the screen through the versus_host group.

Phases

INTRO (1.2 s banner) → PLAY → CLEAR (1.6 s) or a flyby's RESULT (3.2 s) → SHOP if anything can be bought, where the sim stands still until leave_shop() → the next stage. OVER on the last firefly.

The five waves

  1. From the top: 4 ladybirds and, mirrored, 4 gnats (the middle columns).
  2. From the side: the 4 moths, each paired with a ladybird.
  3. From the other side: the remaining 8 ladybirds.
  4. From the top: 8 gnats on the right.
  5. From the top: 8 gnats on the left.

Paths are Catmull-Rom curves through fractions of the field. Each bug walks its path by arc length with a cursor, so a step never re-walks from the start. Bugs in a wave leave 0.12 s apart.

level() = stage − ⌊(stage + 1) / 4⌋ # flybys don't count wave gap = max(2.2, 2.9 − 0.06 · level) s pace (speed) = 1 + 0.045 · min(level, 16) # bugs enter at 120 · pace entry fire = 0 at level ≤ 1, else min(0.5, 0.12 + 0.05 · level) chance of one seed sway = sin(t · 1.15) · 10 while gathering, fading to 0 breathe = spread 1 + 0.13 · (½ − ½cos(t · 1.7)) once seated

The flyby

Stage 3, then every fourth stage (7, 11, 15…). Five waves of eight pass straight through, 3.4 s apart, mixing two kinds each. They never fire and never dive, and every bug takes one shot whatever the stage. Banner: “Flyby! They won't shoot: catch all forty.”

Sim

Dives and seeds

Dives start only once the whole swarm is seated, and only while the firefly is alive. Seats on the swarm's edge are picked before seats in the middle.

next dive in = rand(0.7, 1.3) · max(0.45, (1.6 − 0.1 · level) · (0.6 if ≤ 6 left)) divers at once ≤ min(2 + ⌊level/2⌋, 7) (+2 if ≤ 6 left) who dives : moth 24% · rogue 8% · ladybird 30% · gnat the rest dive speed = 100 · pace; seeds a dive = 0 … min(1 + ⌊level/3⌋, 3)
  • A moth spins a beam (55%) when it has no captive, you are not a pair and no captive or rogue exists. Otherwise it dives with up to two ladybirds from the row below it, within one column.
  • Seeds are aimed where the firefly is and dropped at 30%, 40% and 50% of the field's height. None are dropped within 60 units of the firefly.
  • A bug that flies into the firefly dies with it (rammed). It still pays its points and energy, but it doesn't tap the phone.
  • A diver that leaves the bottom comes back in over the top to its seat.
Sim

The silk beam, the rescue and the pair

  1. Beam run. A lone moth flies down to 54% of the field above the firefly and opens its beam. It grows over 0.8 s, holds to 3.4 s and closes by 4.1 s.
  2. Caught. When the beam is over 60% open, a firefly within 13 × beam units of the moth is caught (“Oh no!”). Shots in the air vanish.
  3. Shot free. Kill the moth while it is still hauling the firefly up (60 u/s, spinning) and it drops free (“Saved!”).
  4. Carried. If the firefly reaches the moth, it is lost and hangs under the moth as a captive. The moth goes home with it.
  5. Rescue. Shoot that moth while it is diving. The captive flies back and docks beside you: a pair (“Double fire!”) that fires from both and is twice the target. A hit costs one half, not a life. If no firefly is alive when it docks, it becomes your firefly and gives the life back.
  6. Rogue. Shoot that moth in its seat instead and the captive turns rose and joins the swarm (500 seated, 1000 diving; “Rogue down!”).

A stray shot on a captive in flight pops it (captive_lost). Only one captive or rogue can exist at a time, and a pair is never beamed.

A bug left with half its health or less is hurt (a moth blushes) and wears a thin bar. Each hit floats what it took (24 numbers at most; a lucky hit is gold with a “!”).

Energy

Every bug shot down pays energy: 1 for a gnat or a ladybird, 2 for a moth or a rogue, plus 10% a level of the Energy card. It is counted internally in orbs (4 to one energy) so fractions carry over. A stage pays about 44 energy, the same every stage. Motes fly to the ENERGY plate on the left of the paper row (the playing thumb hides the right). Energy you don't spend is kept.

The cards

A price moves only when that card is bought. No card has a maximum. Shown in this order:

Lucky hits roll on their own dice (_luck) so they never move the swarm's. The shop card is Peapod's, built again in this screen, not shared: a change to it has to be made in both. Its heading is “Stage N done. Spend your energy on the firefly.” and its button is “Next stage”.

Screen · 2026-09-27

Rewards made loud

arcade/rewards.gd: one sticker-and-bits layer over the screen, shared with Molehill.

  • Every kill throws scraps of the bug's colour and sparks, and letters its points. A moth or a rogue throws gold stars and a ring, and three stars fly home into the score plate.
  • The chain counts kills within 1.5 s of each other. From 3 it shows Chain xN. At 6, 12, 20, 30 and 40 it shows a word: Nice! · Great! · Glowing! · Bug-tastic! · Legendary! From Great! a sunburst plays, and from Bug-tastic! a rain. From 6 a warm glow beats round the field. Losing a firefly breaks the chain.
  • Named moments: Escort bonus, Rogue down!, Saved!, Double fire!, Oh no!, Ouch!, Clear!, the flyby bonus, Extra firefly!, every 10,000 points, and A new best!
  • Sunbursts are added, not laid over (set_additive). Laid over the navy sky, pale gold reads as grey haze.
  • A sticker that would cover a live one lands under it, except the chain count.
  • Reduce motion keeps the words (still) and drops the bits, rays, rain, flash, glow and count.
Screen · 2026-09-27

Look and motion

A night sky (#1d2147 to #4a3f6e) over hedges (#1f3a3a), a moon, dusk glow over a third ridge of hills, swaying grass and flowers, clouds crossing the moon, a star that falls every nine seconds and nine fireflies blinking over the hedge. Score, best and stage sit on a paper row, with the field in a wooden frame.

The firefly

Leans into its movement, thins as it banks, flaps faster, kicks back and flashes with each volley, leaves a wake of lantern motes and breathes a halo. After a respawn it rises from under the hedge and blinks for 1.3 s.

The swarm

Seated bugs bob on their own phase and squash as they land. A hurt moth trembles. A diver wriggles as it peels off. A hit bug is knocked back and swells. Bugs in flight leave a soft streak.

A kill

A flash that shrinks (rather than fades, since fading reads as smoke on the night sky), a thinning ring and flung shards. A moth shakes the field a little; a lost firefly shakes it hard and holds the sim for 0.12 s.

Shots and the beam

Sparks with a tapered trail. Seeds are tumbling rose seeds. The beam is a gradient cone with rim lines, silk motes and a pool of light, and a caught firefly hangs on three threads.

Nothing that only moves is rebuilt to move. The first build laid grass, clouds and stars into the live mesh at 4.65 ms a frame on the Mac. Now the grass is four clumps swayed by skew, the clouds are two meshes slid by transform and the stars are six twinkle groups. The live layers cost about 1 ms. Reduce motion stills the lean, bob, squash, wriggle, streaks, shake, sway, drift and falling star, but keeps the respawn blink.

Haptics: one knock a frame, the strongest

The sim steps twice a frame and a step can pop three bugs, so events ask through _feel(kind) and _knock_now plays the frame's strongest.
