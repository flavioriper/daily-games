01

Krillion no credited developer · krillion.io

Seven prompts a day, one typed answer each, scored by how few other players gave the same valid answer. Rare sinks you deeper.

Loop
Seven category prompts ("Name a word for a baby animal"), identical for everyone, reset 04:00 UTC. One answer per prompt under a countdown. Blank, timed out or rejected answers score zero.
Scoring
Six rarity tiers by share of players who wrote the same thing: Plankton 10, Too Clever 15, Schooler 30, Rare 60, Deep Cut 85, One in a Krillion 100. A perfect day is 700 points, 7,000 m. "Too Clever" catches the second-most-obvious answer, so the game is a read on the crowd, not a quiz.
Share
Depth reached and tier list per prompt. Grading needs everyone's answers, which is why it is one attempt a day.
Retention
Daily Dive, Unlimited, Archive, Themed Packs, My Krillion stats, Friends, merch shop. Dive #64 on 17 Sep puts launch around mid-July 2026.
Why it charts
Same prompts for everyone gives comparison, rarity scoring removes wrong-answer shame, and the depth meter turns a number into a picture people post.
Caveats
Timer reported as 15, 20 or 25 s by different sources. One clone site lists a doubling 4 to 100 point scale. The official site is a JavaScript app and could not be read directly.

02

Size It Up GriddleLink Games (SideQuestTeej) · magnitudle.com/size-it-up

Resize a silhouette until it matches its real size relative to a reference object shown at true scale. Five rounds, closer earns more.

Loop
Each round shows one reference at correct scale and one target. Drag a handle to resize (pan and zoom only move the view), press Lock In, the true size and round score appear. No timer.
Scoring
Up to 100 per round, 500 max, "a closer size estimate earns more points". No win or lose.
Share
Streaks, statistics and leaderboards advertised on the Magnitudle hub. Guest play works, an account carries progress across devices.
Retention
Original, Pop Culture and Geography variants, a free filterable archive, unlimited practice. Free, optional account. Monetisation is a Buy Me a Coffee link.
Why it charts
Playlin: it "turns those wonderfully pointless 'wait, how big is that?' questions into an actual game". Under two minutes, ratio thinking rather than exact numbers. Debuted at #2 in its first week.
Caveats
Per-round thresholds (within 6% = 100, 12% = 90, 20% = 78, 33% = 62, 2× = 45, 3× = 30, 5× or worse = 0) and the five-block coloured share card appear only on clone sites.

03

Number Waffle Waffle Studio Ltd (James Robinson) · wafflegame.net/numberwaffle

The numeric sibling of Waffle: swap digits on a waffle-shaped grid until every row and column holds 1 to 7, in as few swaps as possible.

Loop
Drag any number onto another to swap. Twenty swaps to spend, every puzzle solvable in fifteen. No timer. Ends when solved or when swaps run out ("Oh dear. No stars this time, I'm afraid"). New puzzle at midnight, GMT or local by choice.
Scoring
One star per swap remaining, max five. Fail is zero stars. Green digits sit in the right place, circle clues hold the sum of the digits they point to.
Share
Share and copy button. Stats panel: played, total stars, current and best streak, star distribution 0 to 5. Optional account syncs across devices.
Retention
Part of the Waffle hub with Daily Waffle, weekly Deluxe, supporters-only Royale, ad-gated Archive, puzzle packs of ten, a timed Challenge, and spin-offs. Free with ads, supporters lose the ads.
Why it charts
Playlin: "feels like Sudoku, KenKen, and Waffle got mashed together". It inherits Waffle's audience (around 510,000 weekly players in 2024) and its star-per-spare-swap tension.
Caveats
Exact share text not found. Launch date not found, 19 chart weeks implies roughly May 2026. The mock's grid layout is illustrative.

04

Songless Lessgames · lessgames.com/songless

Name the song from a clip that starts at 0.1 seconds and grows with every wrong guess or skip. A separate daily song per genre channel.

Loop
Pick All, Rock or Hip Hop. Listen, type a guess with autocomplete. Each miss or skip unlocks a longer clip, roughly 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 5 s then the full song. Six guesses, no timer. Ends on the right title or a sixth miss with the answer revealed.
Scoring
Win by naming it within six. Measured in guesses used, no points.
Share
A spoiler-free summary to share, Wordle-style once a day. Local streak, best streak, games played, win rate. No account.
Retention
Daily per genre, free, no signup. Sister games Wordless, Clueless and More/Less on the same hub. Archives exist only on companion and clone sites.
Why it charts
Playlin: "nailing a song from 0.1 seconds feels like a superpower". It fills the hole Heardle left when it closed in 2022.
Caveats
The official page is a JavaScript app that returned no text, so rules come from Playlin and directories. Sources disagree on whether skips spend a guess and whether the first clip is 0.1 or 0.5 s. A $1.99 iOS "Songless" app is third-party.

05

Dartwords The Game Band (Blaseball) · dartwords.com

Guess one secret word in ten darts with no opening clue. Every guess is thrown at a board and lands nearer the bullseye the closer its meaning is, while Darty, a talking dart, drops a conversational hint.

Loop
Free-text word input, one secret word a day, ten darts, no timer found. Broad early hints come from a local language model trained on a dictionary, close-in hints are hand-written. Ends on the word or the tenth dart.
Scoring
Win is the bullseye within ten. Loss screen and any scoring beyond darts used: not found.
Share
Share text, streaks, stats: not found beyond an unattributed snippet saying results are shareable.
Retention
Daily only, free with a tip jar. No archive, unlimited or accounts found. Launched 17 or 18 August 2026 and reached #4 in its second chart week.
Why it charts
A studio with a cult following, a mascot you can blame, and a guessing feel instead of a letter grid. Covered by The Verge and others at launch.
Caveats
"No category, definition or first letter" comes only from a guide site. The mock's hint text and words are invented.

06

4 × 3 Hank Green · hankgreen.com/fourbythree

Nine words, four groups of three, and one hub word that belongs to all four. Find the groups, then the hub is revealed.

Loop
Tap three words and submit, with shuffle and deselect. A correct group locks and clears while the hub stays on the board. A timer runs, hidden by default, with no cap. The optional "Call It" beta lets you name the hub once, wrong means the board fails.
Scoring
Wins start at 100. −15 per mistake, −30 for a slip with two groups left. Bonuses: hub first or middle +20, blue first +5, purple first +15, reverse rainbow +30, under 90 s +30, efficient twelve-click solve +5, +1 per streak day. Hub last in every guess is "Rule Breaker", −100. Perfect is 200 plus streak. Gold and silver plaques under 90 and 120 s.
Share
Results as image or emoji text. Stats: win rate, streak, completed. History lives on the device only, movable with a transfer code.
Retention
Free archive back to 11 June 2026 that does not touch today's game, a community builder with 150+ rated puzzles, accessibility toggles, no accounts, no monetisation found.
Why it charts
Connections' familiarity, a scoring layer for hardcore solvers, and a creator with an enormous audience.
Caveats
Mistake cap and the loss condition without Call It: not found. The mock's word set is invented to illustrate the hub rule.

07

Loopy River Inkwell Games (formerly Lazy River) · inkwellgames.com/games/loopy-river

Draw one continuous river that passes through every cell of a grid exactly once, around walls and islands. Pure deduction, no guessing.

Loop
Drag between cells to draw, drag back to erase, tap to mark an X on impossible paths. Undo, Check and Reset buttons. A timer runs. One puzzle a day at midnight, no guess count. Ends when a single closed loop covers every cell.
Scoring
No points. Solved means one loop with no branches, forks, dead ends or crossings. Difficulty steps up through the week from Monday beginner to Sunday challenge.
Share
Share card, streaks, stats: not found. Subscribers can replay and track their times.
Retention
Free daily with accounts. Subscription at $5.99 a month or $49.99 a year unlocks the archive (since 4 May 2026), replays, early access and solving aids. Weekend puzzles vanish for non-subscribers on Monday. Part of a four-game daily set with Mosaic and others.
Why it charts
A sub-two-minute logic puzzle with exactly one answer, sitting inside a daily ritual of sibling games.
Caveats
Grid size not found. "Exactly two connections per cell" comes only from a guide site. Inkwell's blog says "no account required" and "free means free", which sits awkwardly beside the paywalled archive.

08

Magnitudle GriddleLink Games (SideQuestTeej) · magnitudle.com

One real-world "how many, how big" question a day. Type a number, get scored on how close your order of magnitude lands.

Loop
Numeric input with ×1K to ×1Q scale buttons and a notepad. One question, one guess, no timer. Submit reveals the answer. Survival mode chains questions until you miss by too much.
Scoring
Logarithmic, out of 100. Within a factor of 2 to 3 is 90 to 100, one order of magnitude off is 70 to 80, two orders 50 to 60, three or more below 50. No pass or fail, only a score. Formula not published.
Share
Streak counter on the home page, history page, badges, global leaderboards for Daily, Survival and Size It Up for permanent accounts. Playlin says results compare against other players. Share text format not found.
Retention
Free, ungated archive of every past puzzle across all variants. Guest play with local progress, optional account. No ads or premium, a Buy Me a Coffee link. A blog of Fermi-problem explainers.
Why it charts
One guess, no clue trail, and a forgiving log curve mean anyone with a sensible anchor gets a respectable number. Quick and argument-provoking. The Size It Up spin-off debuted at #2 the same week.
Caveats
Survival specifics (weekly, 10 lives, 15 s per question) come from a third-party listing. The mock's question and answer are illustrative.

09

Vexle VEXLE (David Lawal) · vexle.org · launched 30 Jun 2026

Draw today's country flag from memory on a blank canvas and get a similarity score, or build it from coloured regions in six Wordle-style tries.

Loop
Draw mode: brush, fill and shape tools, undo and clear, no timer found, press Reveal to see your sketch beside the real flag with a percent match. Build mode: paint numbered regions, up to six attempts with per-attempt feedback. Draw Battle: private lobby up to six players, timed, last one standing.
Scoring
Percent based on "getting the major color regions in the right places, not artistic skill". Daily draw earns an S to F grade. 85% or better fills that country in on your world map. Build mode feedback: right colour right spot, right colour wrong spot, colour not on the flag.
Share
The grade is "raised up the flagpole and stamped into a collectible sticker". Share cards carry your sticker, "Share my map" exports the world map, stroke-by-stroke replay videos. Streaks with cross-device sync, a stats page.
Retention
36 themed flag packs (one free), daily bounties of three wanted flags that grant premium access, optional sign-in, ads, premium removes ads and unlocks past dailies and packs. iOS app rated 4.9.
Why it charts
Drawing a flag from memory is instantly funny and shareable. The reveal makes failure entertaining, and the sticker book and map turn it into a collection.
Caveats
Grade thresholds not found. A $19 one-off premium price appears only in search snippets. The mock's drawing and score are invented.

10

Poople Elias Furst · poople.io

Everyone gets the same four-letter start word and must reach POOP by changing one letter at a time through valid English words. Shorter is better.

Loop
Type a four-letter word each step. Exactly one letter changes, no adding, removing or rearranging, and each step must be in the game's dictionary (a solver site counts 2,398 accepted words). No timer. Ends on POOP. One puzzle a day with a delay preventing continuous play.
Scoring
Official: "scored on the length of your path, the quicker you get to poop, the better."
Share
After solving you see a history of your scores and can copy an emoji-grid result, spoiler-free.
Retention
Daily only on the official site, iOS and Android apps with no in-app purchases listed. Ads, accounts, archive: not found. A 2025 Playlin Awards finalist for "Best Game to Test Your Patience". Born from the developer's father's car-trip "to-poop game".
Why it charts
A classic word ladder with a childish target word for a punchline. Fifteen weeks on the chart and a past #1.
Caveats
Step limit, par, stars, streaks and the reset hour appear only on clone sites. The restrictive dictionary is the common complaint. The mock's ladder and share card are illustrative.
