# Satisfactory gameplay spec and data (1.0 through 1.2)

Version context: 1.0 launched Sept 2024. Patch 1.1.0.0 went to Experimental on 2025-04-01 ([wiki](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.0.0)), and 1.1.1.0 (the 1.1 live release) followed on 2025-06-10 ([wiki](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.1.0)). Patch 1.2.0.0 went to Experimental on 2026-03-17 ([wiki](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.2.0.0)). Unless marked otherwise, every number below comes from satisfactory.wiki.gg pages fetched on 2026-09-27, so it reflects the current game.

## Progression: HUB tiers, Space Elevator, MAM, AWESOME Shop, hard drives and alternates

### Takeaway
Progression has four parts:
- **The HUB.** Tiers 0 to 9. Tier 0 is six sequential HUB Upgrades. Tiers 1 to 9 contain 42 milestones in total.
- **The Space Elevator.** Five phases gate the tiers in pairs: phase 1 opens T3-4, phase 2 opens T5-6, phase 3 opens T7-8, phase 4 opens T9, and phase 5 ends the game.
- **The MAM.** Its research trees cover alien and biological resources plus hard drives.
- **The AWESOME Shop.** It spends coupons earned from the AWESOME Sink.

There are 106 alternate recipes. They come from hard drives: 118 crash sites in the world, plus unlimited purchases in the shop after Tier 8.

**Alternate recipes and hard drives.** Source: [Alternate recipes](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Alternate_recipes).
- There are 106 alternate recipes.
- There are 118 crash sites, each holding one hard drive.
- A hard drive is also sold in the AWESOME Shop for 100 coupons after Particle Enrichment (T8), with unlimited purchases. One more comes from the FICSMAS calendar.
- Scanning a drive in the MAM offers a choice of 2 alternates. Each drive can be re-rolled once, so up to 4 options are seen.

- It has 9 sections: FICSIT Specials, Management, Organization, Customizer, Foundations, Walls, Architecture, Equipment, Parts.
- A Hard Drive costs 100 coupons. The Golden Nut Statue costs 1,000, the highest single price.
- Buying everything would take about 11 h 23 m at the theoretical maximum of 184,168,000 points/min.

- Tier 0 has 6 upgrades and Tiers 1 to 9 have 3, 5, 4, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5 and 5 milestones, for 42 milestones outside Tier 0. This is counted from the Milestones page, not stated there.
- Because phase 1 costs only 50 Smart Plating, the effective gates are phases 2 to 4.

### Gaps
- The full MAM node list with costs was not extracted. It has roughly 9 trees with about 10 to 20 nodes each.
- The coupon threshold formula for the AWESOME Sink (points per coupon, which increases over time) was not retrieved.

## Items and resources: raw resources, purities, extraction, overclocking, Somersloops

### Takeaway
- **Solid resources.** There are 10 solid raw resources: Iron, Copper, Caterium, Limestone, Coal, Quartz, Sulfur, Uranium, Bauxite and SAM.
- **Fluid resources.** There are 3 fluid or gas resources: Water, Crude Oil and Nitrogen. Water is also infinite from water bodies. Geysers provide power only.
- **Purity.** Node purity multiplies the base rate by 0.5 (impure), 1 (normal) or 2 (pure).
- **Clock speed.** Clock speed runs up to 250% with 3 Power Shards. Power scales as clock^1.321928, while generators scale linearly.
- **Somersloops.** Each Somersloop adds 25 to 100% of output depending on the machine, and power grows by (1 + filled/total)².

### Cited Findings

**Fluids.**
- Crude oil nodes: 10 impure, 12 normal, 8 pure. The Oil Extractor yields 150, 300 or 600 m³/min by purity.
- Crude oil well satellites: 8 impure, 6 normal, 4 pure. The total crude maximum is 12,600/min ([Resource node](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Resource_node)).
- Geysers: 9 impure, 13 normal, 9 pure (same source).
- The Water Extractor yields 120 m³/min at 20 MW ([Water Extractor](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Water_Extractor)).
- The Resource Well Pressurizer draws 150 MW (up to 503.66 MW at 250%). Each well extractor yields 30, 60 or 120 m³/min per satellite by purity at 100%, and 75, 150 or 300 at 250%. Wells give Crude Oil, Water or Nitrogen Gas ([Resource Well Pressurizer](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Resource_Well_Pressurizer)).
- **Conflict:** the Resource node page lists well extractor rates as 75, 150 or 300 m³/min. Those are the pressurizer's 250% figures, so the 100% base rate is 30, 60 or 120.

**Hand mining.** Manual mining gives 1, 2 or 3 items per tap by purity ([Resource node](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Resource_node)).

**Clock speed.** Source: [Clock speed](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Clock_speed).
- The production power exponent is 1.321928 (log2 of 2.5), changed from 1.6 in Patch 0.7.
- Generator output is linear in clock speed.
- Each Power Shard raises the maximum clock by +50%, for up to 3 shards and a 250% cap.
- Example: the Refinery draws 30 MW at 100%, 75 MW at 200% and 100.73 MW at 250% ([Refinery](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Refinery)).

**Somersloops.** Source: [Production amplifier](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Production_amplifier).
- **Slots per building:**
  - 1 slot (+100%): Smelter, Constructor.
  - 2 slots (+50% each): Assembler, Foundry, Refinery, Converter.
  - 4 slots (+25% each): Manufacturer, Blender, Particle Accelerator, Quantum Encoder.
- **Power:** Power = Base × (1 + filled/total)² × (clock/100)^1.321928. Full amplification is 4x base, and full amplification at 250% is about 13.4x base.
- There are 106 Somersloops in the world as of 1.0.

**Items.** The wiki's Items category has 158 pages ([Items](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Items)). Subcategories:

| Building materials | 42 |
| Crafting components | 86 |

| Project Assembly parts | 13 |

Subcategories overlap.

### Inferences
- The 158 figure is a count of wiki pages, not an in-game item-descriptor count. Fluids and some items may be categorised differently.
- The iron "92,100/min" maximum on the wiki presumably assumes Mk.3 at 250% capped at 780/min per belt, or a similar convention. Recompute from the node counts rather than trusting it.

### Gaps
- Water and Nitrogen well and satellite counts were not retrieved.
- Oil Extractor power was not verified (commonly 40 MW).
- Converter ore-to-ore ratios were only partly retrieved: Reanimated SAM at 10/min, 6 s cycle ([Converter](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Converter)).
- No exact total recipe count was found on [Recipes](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Recipes). Community figures (roughly 200+ standard crafting recipes plus 106 alternates) are unverified.
- The number of Mercer Spheres, Power Slugs and Hard Drive crash sites per biome was not retrieved.

- The Particle Accelerator's draw ramps up over each cycle.

- The search summary's claim of "Manufacturer 40 MW" is wrong. The wiki page says 55 MW.

### Gaps
- Craft Bench and Equipment Workshop were not covered.
- The full buildable count (including logistics and architecture pieces) was not found.

## Power

### Takeaway
Generators:

| Generator | Output |
|---|---|
| Biomass Burner | 30 MW |
| Coal Generator | 75 MW |
| Geothermal | variable, about 100 / 200 / 400 MW average by purity |
| Alien Power Augmenter | 500 MW |
| Fuel Generator | 250 MW |
| Nuclear | 2,500 MW |

Power Storage holds 100 MWh and charges at up to 100 MW.

### Cited Findings

**Generator summary.** Source: [Power](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Power).
- Output figures are as in the table above.
- The Biomass Burner throttles to demand, so it cannot charge Power Storage.
- The Power page gives Geothermal a 50 to 600 MW range with averages of 100, 200 and 400 MW by purity. A third-party calculator instead states 50, 200 and 400 per tier ([xgamingserver](https://xgamingserver.com/tools/satisfactory/power-calculator)). **Treat as conflicting and prefer the wiki.**

**Coal Generator** ([xgamingserver](https://xgamingserver.com/tools/satisfactory/power-calculator), third-party, 1.0-labelled):
- 15 Coal/min, or 7.14 Compacted Coal/min, or 25 Petroleum Coke/min.
- 45 m³/min water.

**Fuel Generator** ([xgamingserver](https://xgamingserver.com/tools/satisfactory/power-calculator)):
- 250 MW.
- 20/min of Fuel or Liquid Biofuel, 7.5/min Turbofuel, 4.17/min Rocket Fuel, 3/min Ionized Fuel.
- No water.

**Nuclear Power Plant** ([Nuclear Power Plant](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Nuclear_Power_Plant)): 2,500 MW and 240 m³/min water for every fuel.

| Fuel rod | Rods/min | Burn time | Waste/min |
|---|---|---|---|
| Uranium | 0.2 | 300 s | 10 Uranium Waste |
| Plutonium | 0.1 | 600 s | 1 Plutonium Waste |
| Ficsonium | 1 | 60 s | none |

**Power Storage** ([Power Storage](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Power_Storage)):
- 100 MWh, charges at up to 100 MW, discharge unlimited.
- Unlocked in T4 Expanded Power Infrastructure.
- Costs 5 Encased Industrial Beam, 10 Modular Frame, 100 Wire.

**1.2 change.** Power lines can daisy-chain, with two connections per building ([Patch 1.2.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.2.0.0)).

### Inferences
- The fuel rates imply these energy values: Coal 300 MJ, Compacted Coal 630 MJ, Petroleum Coke 180 MJ, Fuel 750 MJ/m³, Turbofuel 2,000, Rocket Fuel 3,600, Ionized Fuel 5,000. Each is derived as rate = MW × 60 / MJ.

### Gaps
- Biomass Burner per-fuel rates were not retrieved.
- Alien Power Augmenter details were not retrieved: its grid-wide percentage boost, and the extra boost it gives when fuelled with Synthetic Power Shards.
- Power Pole connection counts were not retrieved.

## Logistics

### Takeaway
- **Belts.** Mk.1 to Mk.6 carry 60, 120, 270, 480, 780 and 1,200 items/min.
- **Pipes.** Mk.1 carries 300 m³/min and Mk.2 carries 600 m³/min.
- **Trains.** The Electric Locomotive draws 25 to 110 MW and reaches about 120 km/h.
- **Drones.** Drone Ports draw 100 MW and hold 18 slots in and 18 out. Drones burn fuel by distance, at 50 to 100 m/s.
- **1.1 additions.** Priority Merger, hypertube junctions, Personnel Elevator, Conveyor Wall Hole, and splitters on lifts.
- **1.2 additions.** Rebuilt vehicle paths, fluid trucks, and a pipeline T-junction.

### Cited Findings

**Belts.** Source: [Conveyor Belts](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Conveyor_Belts).

| Mark | Items/min | Unlocked | Player speed on belt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mk.1 | 60 | T0 | 1.2 m/s |
| Mk.2 | 120 | T2 | 2.4 m/s |
| Mk.3 | 270 | T4 | 5.4 m/s |
| Mk.4 | 480 | T5 | 9.6 m/s |
| Mk.5 | 780 | T7 | 15.6 m/s |
| Mk.6 | 1,200 | T9 | 24 m/s |

- A segment runs from about 0.5 to 56 m, with a maximum slope of 35°.
- Lift marks match the belt marks ([Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones)).

**Pipes.** Source: [Pipelines](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Pipelines).
- Mk.1 carries 300 m³/min and costs 1 Copper Sheet.
- Mk.2 carries 600 m³/min and costs 2 Copper Sheet + 1 Plastic.
- Segments run 1 to 56 m.
- Gases need no pumps.

**Trains.** Source: [Electric Locomotive](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Electric_Locomotive).
- The locomotive draws 25 MW idle and up to 110 MW. Regenerative braking returns up to 33 MW.
- Top speed is 120 km/h on flat track.
- One locomotive pulls about 100 empty cars on flat ground.
- Rail segments are 12 to 100 m and cost 1 Steel Pipe + 1 Steel Beam ([Railway](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Railway)).
- 1.1 made buffer stops required at rail ends ([Patch 1.1.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.0.0)).
- Signals are Block and Path, from T6 Railway Signalling ([Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones)).

**Drones.** Source: [Drone Port](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Drone_Port).
- A port draws 100 MW constantly.
- A port has 18-slot input and 18-slot output inventories. A drone carries 9 slots.
- Fuels: Battery, Packaged Fuel, Turbofuel, Rocket Fuel, Ionized Fuel, Uranium Fuel Rod, Plutonium Fuel Rod.
- Speed is 50 m/s on Packaged Fuel and 100 m/s on Ionized Fuel or Plutonium rods.
- Fuel is consumed only by distance flown.

**Vehicles.**
- The Truck Station and Tractor come at T3, the Truck at T5, and the Explorer from MAM Quartz ([Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones), [MAM](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/MAM)).
- 1.2 remade vehicle path automation so that paths are built like railways. It added Fluid Truck Stations and Fluid Trucks with 3,200 m³ capacity at T5 ([Patch 1.2.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.2.0.0)).

**Splitters and mergers.**
- Splitters, mergers and lifts come at T1. Smart and Programmable splitters come from MAM Caterium ([MAM](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/MAM)).
- 1.1 added the Priority Merger, splitters and mergers attachable to lifts, and the Conveyor Wall Hole. The 1.1 live notes also mention vertical belt splitters and mergers and conveyor throughput counters ([Patch 1.1.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.0.0); [comicbook summary of 1.1](https://comicbook.com/gaming/news/satisfactory-update-1-1-patch-notes-details/)).

**Hypertubes.** They unlock at T4. 1.1 added three-way junctions and branches ([Patch 1.1.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.0.0)).

## Other systems: world, combat, equipment, blueprints, depot, story, versions

### Takeaway
- **World.** About 8 × 6.8 km with 21 named biomes, 4 of them starting areas.
- **Other systems.** Blueprints come in three designer sizes (Mk.1 to Mk.3). The Dimensional Depot is unlocked through MAM Alien Technology.
- **1.1.** Controller support, photo mode, Personnel Elevator and more ADA dialogue.
- **1.2.** Weather, the vehicle path overhaul, fluid trucks, Game Modes, SPWN, and Unreal Engine 5.6.1.

### Cited Findings

**World.** Source: [World](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/World).
- The playable area is 7.972 × 6.8 km, about 47.1 km². The official description gives roughly 5.4 × 5.4 km for the landmass.
- There are 21 named biomes, including 4 start areas: Grass Fields, Rocky Desert, Northern Forest and Dune Desert.
- There are more than 52 caves.

**Equipment.** Source: [Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones), [MAM](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/MAM).
- From milestones: Portable Miner, Object Scanner, Chainsaw, Xeno-Basher, Jetpack, Hoverpack, Hazmat Suit.
- From the MAM: Blade Runners, Parachute, Gas Mask, Rifle, Rebar Gun, Nobelisk and Detonator, Zipline, inhalers.

**Blueprints.** Designers Mk.1, Mk.2 and Mk.3 unlock at T4, T6 and T9 ([Milestones](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Milestones)). 1.1 added blueprint auto-connect for conveyors, pipes and railways ([Patch 1.1.0.0](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/Patch_1.1.0.0)).

**Endgame.**
- Main and Satellite Portals come from T9 Spatial Energy Regulation.
- The Alien Power Augmenter and Dimensional Depot Uploader come from MAM Alien Technology ([MAM](https://satisfactory.wiki.gg/wiki/MAM)).
